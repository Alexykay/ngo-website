The Team

Albert Ayirebi Acquah

View Bio

Albert Neenyi Ayirebi- Acquah is a strategic finance leader with over two decades of experience, currently serving as Commercial Director at CENIT Energy Limited. As Board Chair of Sankofa Earth Foundation, he leverages his deep expertise in governance and sustainable energy to champion climate-positive ddevelopment in Ghana.

Mohammed Nazifi Abubakari

View Bio

Mohammed Nazifi Abubakar is a Board Member at Sankofa and an Occupational Behavioural Therapist. He brings expertise in operations and logistics management, with experience coordinating logistics and international travel between Ghana and the United States to support efficient organizational operations and strategic initiatives.

Rodney Fowlkes

View Bio

Rodney Fowlkes is a Senior Consultant at JEP Communications and a General Board Member. A veteran IT leader with over 37 years of experience-including 20 years as CIO across HBCU's, major universities,and the corporate sector-he brings deep technical expertise to his roles. Beyond his career, Rodney actively supports local communities by serving on non-profit boards, judging youth computer science competitions, and delivering essential technology solutions to communities and organizations.

Nana Mariam Yussif

View Bio

Nana Mariam Yussif is a climate finance and gender policy srtategist serving as the Ghana Country Director for the Women Environmental Programme (WEP). With six years of experience across climate adaptation, policy, and just transition, she leads community-based initiatives that have impacted over 600 women, youth, and vulnerable groups. She specializes in translating global climate commitments into locally driven, bottom-up solutions to advance sustainable development across Africa.

Adjoa Achiaa Kesse-Sam (AJ)

View Bio

Adjoa Achiaa Kesse-Sam (AJ) fondly called AJ, is a climate advocate and the Coordinator at Sankofa Earth Foundation. With over five years of experience in renewable energy, sustainable development, community engagement, and just energy transition advocacy. Drawing on her expertise in business management and administration, she leads community-driven initiatives to promote a just, inclusive energy transition- focusing on empowering women, youth, and undeserved populations to build climate resilience and sustainable livelihoods.

Monica Nyarko
